# PPodcast 1.0.0

The first stable version of PPodcast, a focused audio podcast player for Android
10 and newer. Follow your shows, arrange a listening queue, and stream episodes
or download them for later.

## Features

- Add shows with an HTTPS RSS address or an Apple Podcasts link. Import and
  export subscriptions with OPML.
- Browse new episodes, favorites, listening history and unfinished episodes.
- Build and reorder your queue, with automatic playback of the next episode.
- Use chapters, per-show playback speed, adjustable skip controls and a sleep
  timer that stops after a chosen time or at the end of an episode.
- Download episodes manually or automatically, set a storage budget and choose
  whether to remove completed downloads. Download before playing saves the
  complete episode before starting audio.
- Schedule feed refreshes and downloads for selected days and a local start
  time. Android may delay background work, so allow time before departure.
- Use Offline mode to stop all app networking while keeping downloaded audio,
  saved information and cached covers available.
- Choose a light or dark appearance. No app account is needed; subscriptions,
  downloads and listening history stay on your device.

## Refined during preview testing

- Fixed queue autoplay, including Download before playing, while retaining
  Offline-mode and sleep-timer limits. An unavailable next episode stays queued.
- Podcast audio pauses when another app takes audio focus. Temporary
  interruptions pause speech and resume when focus returns unless you paused
  manually. Starting other media leaves the podcast paused until you press Play.
- Simplified Add podcast and playback controls, separated favorite/queue
  highlights, and gave New episodes a distinct navigation icon.
- Highlighted the current queue item, with a violet accent in dark mode. Up next
  appears near the end; action-required warnings appear immediately and empty
  queues stay quiet.
- Improved download compatibility with feeds that omit size hints or serve audio
  through longer redirect chains.

## Updating

PPodcast was previously named PlainPodDroid. The application ID and signing
identity are unchanged; updates retain your subscriptions, downloads and
settings. Existing APK filenames and repository links keep the previous name.
Android version code: 4.

The app plays HTTPS audio podcasts from supplied feeds; it has no podcast
directory or cross-device sync. Podcast publishers may include ads in their
audio. Google Play Services are not required.
